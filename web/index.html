Limits and supported files

DOCX only. Password-protected and digitally signed documents cannot be processed. Maximum expanded size: 250 MiB, with up to 5,000 entries and 50 MiB per entry. Images above 25 megapixels are kept unchanged.

Image report

Each image is kept in its original format. A replacement is used only when it is smaller.
